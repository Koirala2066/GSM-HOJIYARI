Owner's Story

Sesh Kant Koirala

Sesh Kant Koirala was born into a humble family in the Gandaki region as the youngest of three brothers. His father served the community for many years, and Sesh remembers a childhood shaped by simplicity and strong family ties. To support his family and seek better opportunities, he travelled abroad as a trainee worker and lived many years away from home.

Life abroad was not easy — it meant long hours, hard work, and the pain of being separated from loved ones. Still, he studied, learned new skills and even learned the local language to improve his chances. After years of perseverance, he returned to Nepal with experience, hope, and a determination to give back to his community.

After roughly ten years away, he came back to Nepal and started GSM HOJIYARI UDHYOG .PVT.LTD to create jobs, support local families and produce high-quality Nepal-made products. His journey is a story of sacrifice, resilience, and the dream of building something lasting for future generations.

In recognition of his dedication and the skills he acquired while working in Korea, he was honored with an award from the Government of the Republic of Korea for his outstanding work in applying those skills to run and grow his company in Nepal.

"Hard work, faith and responsibility to my family are what brought me home — to build, to employ, and to serve."
